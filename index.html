FO-4.1 • Q2(i)

Diagonals 8 cm Intersecting at 30°

📋 Construction Task / Problem

Draw a quadrilateral whose diagonals have equal lengths of 8 cm that bisect each other and intersect at an angle of 30°.

💡 Geometric Concept & Principle

Diagonals bisecting each other form a parallelogram. Since diagonals are equal in length and bisect each other at given angles, they define symmetric quadrilateral configurations.
