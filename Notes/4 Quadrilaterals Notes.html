Complete Study Guide & Notes

4.1 Rectangles and Squares

A quadrilateral is a four-sided figure. The word 'quadrilateral' is derived from Latin words - quadri meaning four, and latus referring to sides.

Rectangle Definition
A rectangle is a quadrilateral in which the angles are all right angles (\(90^\circ\)) and the opposite sides are of equal length.

Properties of a Rectangle:

  • All the angles of a rectangle are \(90^\circ\).
  • The opposite sides of a rectangle are equal.
  • The opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel to each other.
  • The diagonals of a rectangle are of equal length and they bisect each other.
Square Definition
A square is a quadrilateral in which all the angles are equal to \(90^\circ\), and all the sides are of equal length.

Properties of a Square: Since a square is a special type of rectangle, all properties of a rectangle hold true. It also has these properties:

  • All the sides of a square are equal to each other.
  • The opposite sides of a square are parallel to each other.
  • The diagonals of a square are of equal length and they bisect each other at \(90^\circ\).
  • The diagonals of a square bisect the angles of the square.

4.2 Angles in a Quadrilateral

Is it possible to construct a quadrilateral with three angles equal to \(90^\circ\) and the fourth angle not equal to \(90^\circ\)?

Angle Sum Property
The sum of all angles in any quadrilateral is \(360^\circ\).

Because the sum must be \(360^\circ\), if three angles are \(90^\circ\) (sum = \(270^\circ\)), the fourth must be \(90^\circ\). Therefore, a quadrilateral cannot have only three right angles.

Figure it Out (Page 94)

Q1. Find all the other angles inside the given rectangles.
Solution:

Refer to the properties of a rectangle. Let's look at the first rectangle with a given angle of \(30^\circ\) between the diagonal and a side.

Step 1: The corner angle of a rectangle is \(90^\circ\). If one part is \(30^\circ\), the other part is \(90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ\).
Step 2: Opposite sides are parallel, so alternate interior angles are equal. If the angle at the bottom left is \(30^\circ\), the alternate angle at the top right is also \(30^\circ\).
Step 3: Diagonals bisect each other and are equal, creating isosceles triangles. This means the base angles of these triangles are equal.
Final Answer: The angles inside the triangles formed by the diagonals will be pairs of \(30^\circ, 30^\circ, 120^\circ\) and \(60^\circ, 60^\circ, 60^\circ\).
Q4. We have seen how to get \(90^\circ\) using paper folding. Now, suppose we do not have any paper but two sticks of equal length, and a thread. How do we make an exact \(90^\circ\) using these?
Solution:
Step 1: Let AB and CD be the equal sticks. Put them together such that their midpoints coincide at O.
Step 2: Now fix one end of the thread at A, pass it through C, and extend it to B.
Step 3: The figure ACBD formed is a rectangle because its diagonals are equal (the sticks) and bisect each other (at their midpoints).
Final Answer: Because ACBD is a rectangle, the angle formed at the corner (e.g., \(\angle C\)) is exactly \(90^\circ\).

4.3 More Quadrilaterals with Parallel Opposite Sides

Are there quadrilaterals that have parallel opposite sides that are not rectangles? Yes, they are called parallelograms.

Parallelogram Definition
A parallelogram is a quadrilateral in which opposite sides are parallel.

Properties of a Parallelogram:

  • The opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.
  • The opposite sides of a parallelogram are parallel.
  • In a parallelogram, the adjacent angles add up to \(180^\circ\), and the opposite angles are equal.
  • The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.

4.4 Quadrilaterals with Equal Sidelengths

Are squares the only quadrilaterals that have equal sidelengths? No, a quadrilateral in which all the sides have the same length is a rhombus.

Rhombus Definition
A rhombus is a quadrilateral in which all the sides have the same length.
Note: Every rhombus is a parallelogram, so all properties of a parallelogram apply.

Properties of a Rhombus:

  • All the sides of a rhombus are equal to each other.
  • The opposite sides of a rhombus are parallel to each other.
  • In a rhombus, the adjacent angles add up to \(180^\circ\), and the opposite angles are equal.
  • The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.
  • The diagonals of a rhombus bisect its angles.

Figure it Out (Page 102)

Q1. Find the remaining angles in the quadrilateral (i) PAR E, given it's a parallelogram and angle P is \(40^\circ\).
Solution:
Step 1: In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal.
Step 2: Therefore, \(\angle A = \angle P = 40^\circ\).
Step 3: Adjacent angles add up to \(180^\circ\).
Step 4: So, \(\angle E = 180^\circ - 40^\circ = 140^\circ\). And \(\angle R = \angle E = 140^\circ\).
Final Answer: \(\angle E = 140^\circ\), \(\angle R = 140^\circ\), and \(\angle A = 40^\circ\).
Q1. Find the remaining angles in the quadrilateral (iii) X U V W, given it's a rhombus, and angle U V W is split by a diagonal, with one part being \(30^\circ\).
Solution:
Step 1: The diagonals of a rhombus bisect its angles. If half the angle is \(30^\circ\), the full angle \(\angle UVW = 60^\circ\).
Step 2: Opposite angles are equal, so \(\angle WXU = \angle UVW = 60^\circ\).
Step 3: Because the diagonal bisects the angle, \(\angle UXV = \angle WXV = 30^\circ\).
Step 4: Adjacent angles sum to \(180^\circ\). So, \(\angle U = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ\).
Step 5: Opposite angles are equal, so \(\angle W = \angle U = 120^\circ\).
Final Answer: \(\angle WXU = 60^\circ\), \(\angle UXV = \angle WXV = 30^\circ\), \(\angle U = 120^\circ\), \(\angle W = 120^\circ\).

4.5 & 4.6 Kite, Trapezium & Summary

Kite Definition
A kite is a quadrilateral with two non-overlapping adjacent pairs of sides having the same length.
Trapezium Definition
A trapezium is a quadrilateral having at least one pair of parallel opposite sides.

When the non-parallel sides of a trapezium have the same lengths, the trapezium is called an isosceles trapezium. In an isosceles trapezium, the angles opposite to the equal sides are equal.

Summary of Quadrilateral Relationships:
* Every square is a rectangle and a rhombus.
* Every rectangle and rhombus is a parallelogram.
* The sum of the angle measures in a quadrilateral is \(360^\circ\).

Final Exercises (Page 107 - 109)

Q4. Draw a Venn diagram showing the set of parallelograms, kites, rhombuses, rectangles, and squares. Then, answer: (i) What is the quadrilateral that is both a kite and a parallelogram?
Solution:
Step 1: A kite has equal adjacent sides. A parallelogram has equal opposite sides.
Step 2: A figure that is both must have all sides equal.
Final Answer: Rhombus and Square.
Q8. If a quadrilateral has four equal sides and one angle of \(90^\circ\), will it be a square?
Solution:
Step 1: A quadrilateral with four equal sides is a rhombus.
Step 2: In a rhombus, opposite angles are equal and adjacent angles are supplementary (add up to \(180^\circ\)).
Step 3: If one angle is \(90^\circ\), the adjacent angle must be \(180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ\). The opposite angle must also be \(90^\circ\).
Step 4: All four angles are equal to \(90^\circ\).
Final Answer: Yes, it will be a square.
Q11. State whether the following statement is true or false. Justify your answer. (iii) A quadrilateral whose diagonals bisect each other must be a parallelogram.
Solution:
Step 1: Recall the properties of a parallelogram.
Step 2: One of the defining properties (Deduction 8) is that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
Final Answer: True.
